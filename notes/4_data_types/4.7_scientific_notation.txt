4.7 — Introduction to scientific notation =========================================================================================================================================

Shorthand for large numbers in the form of significand * 10 ^ exponent, for example:

1.2 * 10^4 = 12,000

The way this works is simply by moving the comma 4 places to the right.

If you have an incredibly large number like the mass of an electron: 9.1093837e-31 kg you would move the decimal 31 places to the left

Significant digits-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

If given the number 3.14 as the only significant digits you would experss it like this: 3.14e0 
You also want to trim off leading and trailing zeros if they arent significant


Examples----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You noramlly want to try to get rid of the leading zeros as opposed to the trailing

Start with: 600.410
Slide decimal point left 2 spaces: 6.00410e2
No leading or trailing 0s
Left with: 6.00410e2 (6 significant digits)

Start with: 0.0078900
Slide decimal point 3 spaces to the right: 0007.8900e-3
Tirm leading/trailing 0s: 7.8900e-3 (5 significant digits)

Starts with: 42030 (no info saying the trailing 0 is significant)
Slide decimal 4 spaces to the left: 4.2030e4
trim leading/trailing 0s: 4.203e4
Left with 4.203e4 (4 significant digits)

Start with 42030 (assume trailing zero is significant)
Slide decimal 4 spaces the left: 4.2030e4
Keep trailing zero 
Left with 4.2030e4
